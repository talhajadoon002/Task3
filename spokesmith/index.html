Two mechanics, one van, a lot of broken chains

Spokesmith started in 2019 when two workshop mechanics got tired of customers leaving bikes for a week over a ten-minute fix. We bought a second-hand van, bolted a workbench into it and started driving to people instead.

Today we are a team of six, all certified, all paid to take the time a repair needs. We repair commuter bikes, e-bikes, kids' bikes and the odd cargo bike.

What is in the van

A portable stand, tyres and tubes in 20 common sizes, brake pads, chains, cables, and a card reader. If we need a part we do not carry, we order it and come back at no extra visit fee.
